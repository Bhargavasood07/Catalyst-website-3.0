Build Weekend is our biggest event of the year. Thirty hours, one theme, and a room full of people trying to make something genuinely useful.

## Theme

**Agents for campus.** Timetable assistants, lab-booking bots, accessibility tools, a library search that actually understands what you mean. If it helps a student or staff member, it fits.

## Format

- Teams of 2 to 4. Solo applicants are matched into teams on day one.
- Compute credits for every team.
- Judging on usefulness, technical depth and the demo itself.
