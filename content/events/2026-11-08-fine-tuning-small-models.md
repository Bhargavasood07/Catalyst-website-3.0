This is our flagship beginner workshop, and the one most members say made things click.

## What you'll do

1. Load a small open-weights model and see what it gets wrong.
2. Build a tiny instruction dataset together from college notices and FAQs.
3. Train a LoRA adapter on a shared GPU and compare before and after.
4. Push your adapter to the Hugging Face Hub so you can keep using it.

## Before you come

- Create a free [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co) account.
- Basic Python is enough. You don't need any ML background.
